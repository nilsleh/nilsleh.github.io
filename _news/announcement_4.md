New preprint that has been fun to explore with fantastic collaborators: [*Beyond Accuracy: Assessing Calibration of Geospatial Foundation Models and Their Sensitivity to Distribution Shifts*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16614). We evaluate the sensitivity of geospatial foundation models to distribution shifts, to more holistically assess their performance beyond accuracy.
